## Joining and leaving

There is one maintainer today. The rule in force is the change controller rule of `GOVERNANCE.md`: a change to this table is a pull request approved by the change controller. The model with more than one maintainer is the one of `GOVERNANCE.md` (decided 2026-10-15): when two independent implementations claim conformance, change control is proposed for transfer to a neutral foundation, and this section is replaced by its rule, as in `MAINTAINERS.md` of A2A and of MCP.
