# Maintainers

Maintainers are accountable for the JOS specification: they review changes under [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), cut releases, and answer security reports under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## How to reach maintainers

- Public matters: open an issue using the templates in `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`.
- Security: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (private vulnerability report on GitHub). Never open a public issue for an exploitable problem.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor becomes a maintainer by nomination of an existing maintainer after sustained, reviewed contributions to the specification, the schema, or the conformance material, and by accepting this document, the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and the change control rules in GOVERNANCE.md. Nominations are recorded as a pull request that edits this table.

## Stepping down

A maintainer steps down by opening a pull request that removes their row. Maintainers inactive for twelve months may be moved to emeritus by the remaining maintainers.
