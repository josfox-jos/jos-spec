# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the JOS specification community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: demonstrating empathy and kindness, being respectful of differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community and the specification.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without explicit permission, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to any maintainer through the contact listed there. All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and maintainers respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant community impact guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
